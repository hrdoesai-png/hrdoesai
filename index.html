How it works

The HR Does AI Transformation Framework

Six moves, in order. The tool comes after the work is worth automating.

  1. 01

    Discover

    You get an inventory of the HR work before any tool is chosen.

  2. 02

    Redesign

    You get a defined future operating model for that work.

  3. 03

    Govern

    You get permitted uses, data rules, and human accountability.

  4. 04

    Build

    You get the workflows, prompts, and templates for the redesigned work.

  5. 05

    Adopt

    You get training so people can use what was built.

  6. 06

    Measure

    You get tracking of time, quality, capacity, and risk.
